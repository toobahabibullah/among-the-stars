About this project

About & Sources

Who we are, where our facts come from, and the promises we make to our readers.

Our purpose

Among the Stars is an educational website for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, answering the challenge “Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA’s Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars.”

Every machine has a mission. Every mission leaves a story. We connect a place on a revolving globe with a short, accurate story about each machine: why it was sent, how it worked, what scientists learned, and what happened to it.

The site is written for curious learners aged about 10–14, in the friendly voice of a museum guide. Short sentences. Real facts. No made-up discoveries.

The team

Among the Stars is a volunteer team that built The Forgotten Space Museum for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. We wanted to give the machines left on the Moon, on Mars and in deep space a place where their stories can be found.

Our accuracy promises

  • Every exhibit is built from our checked data file — never from guesses.
  • Anything still being checked is marked “Coming soon” or “This information is being fact-checked.”
  • We use precise mission status labels, each with a plain-language definition and a NASA source, listed in the Mission status labels section below.
  • If a machine “speaks” in the first person, we label it “A storytelling voice”.
  • Every exhibit shows its sources and image credits.

Privacy & offline use

No accounts. No logins. No tracking. No chatbot. The whole site runs from the files in this folder — open it with a double-click or a simple local server, and it works with no internet connection.

Motion is calm by design. If your device asks for reduced motion, the site turns off auto-rotation and fly-to animations automatically.
